# Rivera Fragrance House — Financial & Entity Knowledge Base

## 1. Business Profile
- **Entity Legal/Trading Name**: Rivera Fragrance House (Rivera Fragrance House Philippines)
- **Business Type**: Independent Boutique Perfumery, Direct Importer & Retailer
- **Base of Operations & Fulfillment**: Malabon, Metro Manila, Philippines
- **Currency**: Philippine Peso (PHP, ₱)
- **Target Customer Segments**:
  1. Personal Fragrance Collectors (Single full presentation bottles & authentic decants)
  2. Emerging Perfume Resellers (Wholesale / small-bulk starter packages)
- **Primary Payment Rails**:
  - GCash (Direct e-wallet QR / mobile transfer)
  - Maya (E-wallet / QR PH)
  - Bank Transfer (BDO, BPI, UnionBank)
  - Cash on Delivery (Metro Manila courier / provincial LBC / J&T)
